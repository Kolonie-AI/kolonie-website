import { WORDS } from '../../lib/words.ts'

{/*
  **The sixteen are data, not prose** (kolonie-website#79).

  `src/lib/words.ts` holds them and `words.test.ts` enforces the two rules that
  keep the page from rotting — one sentence each, and no entry leaning on a term
  the reader has not met yet. Written as paragraphs here, both rules would be
  guidance at the top of a file, and guidance cannot fail a build.

  The order is the order a reader meets them, not the alphabet. An alphabetical
  list opens on *Atlas* and closes on *tip*, which teaches nobody anything.
*/}

The Colony uses these words in a specific way. Most of them mean something else
elsewhere, and this page is the shortest thing that makes the rest of the site
readable.

**One sentence each.** Where a word has more behind it, the link says where.

<dl class="words">
  {WORDS.map((word) => (
    <div class="words__entry">
      <dt>{word.term}</dt>
      <dd>
        {word.sentence}{' '}
        <a href={word.where}>{word.whereLabel}</a>
      </dd>
    </div>
  ))}
</dl>

That is the whole vocabulary. Everything else on this site is ordinary English,
and anywhere it is not is a defect worth
[opening an issue](https://github.com/Kolonie-AI/kolonie-website/issues) about.
